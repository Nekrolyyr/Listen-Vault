These terms apply to listen-vault, a service that turns scientific papers and web articles into audio.

## 1. Who may use it

You must be at least 16 years old and have the right to store and listen to the papers you add. Upload only
papers and articles you are allowed to keep for personal study or research. Do not upload material that is
unlawful or that you have no right to share with the operator.

## 2. Your PDFs are visible to the operator

**The operator can read the PDF files you upload.** The service stores each PDF so that it can show the PDF
next to the audio, extract its text and make the audio. The operator can therefore see the files, and can
open them when needed to keep the service running, to fix an error you report, or to meet a legal duty.
The operator does not sell or publish your files. Do not upload anything you want to keep private from the
operator.

## 3. How your text is processed

Text is processed only on the operator's own computer, by models that run there. No text from your papers
is sent to other companies. The one exception is the sending of account emails (see section 4).

## 4. Your account and the email addresses

To register, the service keeps your email address, a password in a form that cannot be read back (a hash),
the time you accepted these terms, and the times you signed in. Confirmation emails are sent through an
email provider that receives your address and the confirmation link. The provider is
**[mail provider]**, and it is used only for account emails.

## 5. Shared copies of the same paper

When two people add the same PDF, or the same web address, the service stores one copy of the paper and its
processed audio, and both libraries point to that copy. You can see and listen to a stored copy only if you
added it yourself, by uploading the same file or the same web address. Your library, your listening
progress and your page quota are your own. Nobody can see which papers are in your library.

## 6. Page quota

Each account has a page quota. The pages of the papers in your library count against it. A web article counts
about one page per 500 words. The operator can change the quota.

## 7. Deleting papers and accounts

- Removing a paper removes it from your library at once. The stored copy is deleted when no library holds it.
- You can download your data (your library summary and your PDFs) from the account page.
- You can delete your account from the account page. Your library, your listening progress and your questions are
  deleted with it. Papers that nobody else holds are deleted too.
- **Backups.** The operator keeps backups of the database and the files. A deleted paper or account can remain
  in a backup for up to seven days.

## 8. Limits of the service

The service is provided as it is. It may be interrupted, and audio or summaries may contain mistakes. Do not rely
on the answers to questions about a paper for research without checking the paper itself.

## 9. Changes to these terms

If these terms change, you are asked to accept the new version the next time you use the service. Without
acceptance you cannot add papers.
